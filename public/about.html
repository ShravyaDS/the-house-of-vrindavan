About Us

Corporate gifting, thoughtfully selected.

The House of Vrindavan is a Bengaluru-based premium corporate gifting company with a simple approach: bring together a thoughtfully selected collection of quality products that businesses can confidently gift to employees, clients and channel partners.

Our Name

Why "The House of Vrindavan"

Vrindavan has long been associated with abundance, care and hospitality. We borrow that spirit through a collection that feels considered, useful and appropriate for corporate relationships.

Our Belief

Less choice. Better choice.

We believe great corporate gifting begins with great product selection. Our portfolio spans Bags & Travel, Gourmet & Festive, and Joining & Writing Essentials.

What Sets Us Apart

Curated Collection
